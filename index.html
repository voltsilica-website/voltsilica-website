About VOLTSILICA TECHNOLOGIES

VOLTSILICA TECHNOLOGIES focuses on practical engineering education and industry-oriented technical skill development.

Our training areas include semiconductor fundamentals, VLSI Physical Design, embedded systems, electronics, IoT and hardware project development.

We aim to connect classroom concepts with practical tools and hands-on learning through technical workshops and structured training activities.
